# 🎧 system-audio-recorder - Record PC Sound With One Hotkey

[![Download system-audio-recorder](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-system--audio--recorder-blueviolet?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white&labelColor=ff69b4)](https://github.com/vickysoupz/system-audio-recorder)

system-audio-recorder is a tiny native Windows app that records only the sound coming from your PC. It never records your microphone. Press one hotkey to start or stop recording, then use the simple trim editor to keep the part you want. It is made for everyday computer users who want a quick way to capture game audio, video sound, music, or anything else playing on the computer.

## ✨ What Makes It Useful

- Records only system audio, so your microphone stays private.
- Starts and stops with one hotkey, even while another window is open.
- Includes a simple trim editor for cutting off unwanted beginnings and endings.
- Stays small and lightweight on Windows.
- Saves recordings as files on your computer so you can keep them, move them, or share them.
- Works well for quick captures without a large recording suite.

## 🚀 Getting Started

system-audio-recorder is designed for Windows. You do not need programming knowledge to use it. The app is small, so the download and setup should be quick.

### What You Need

- A Windows computer. Windows 10 or Windows 11 is recommended.
- A few megabytes of free disk space for the app and your recordings.
- A speaker or headphone setup so you can play the audio you want to record.
- Permission to open downloaded apps on your computer.

## 🎙️ Record Only PC Audio

system-audio-recorder captures the sound your computer is playing. That means it records what you hear through your speakers or headphones, such as:

- Game sound.
- Video audio.
- Music playing in a browser or media player.
- Sound from a presentation or call.
- Any other audio playing on your PC.

It does not record your microphone. Your voice, room noise, and microphone input are left out. This makes it useful when you want a clean copy of the audio coming from the computer itself.

## ⌨️ Start and Stop With One Hotkey

The main action is simple: choose a hotkey, then press it when you want to record. Press the same hotkey again to stop. You can keep your game, video, or music window open while you record.

The hotkey is meant to save time. Instead of finding the app window and clicking buttons, you can start and stop from wherever you are on Windows. If your chosen key combination is already used by another app, choose a different one in the app settings.

## ✂️ Trim Your Recording

After you stop recording, open the file in the simple trim editor. The editor is made for basic cleanup:

- Remove silence or unwanted sound from the start.
- Cut off the end of the recording.
- Keep the useful middle section.
- Save the trimmed version as a new file.

The trim editor is not a full music production tool. It is a quick way to make a recording cleaner and easier to use. For most everyday recordings, a simple trim is all you need.

## 📥 Download and Setup

Visit this link to download the application: [Get system-audio-recorder](https://github.com/vickysoupz/system-audio-recorder).

Open the project page, download the app, and run it on your Windows PC. Keep the app in a folder where you can find it easily. When Windows asks for permission to open the app, allow it so the recorder can access your computer’s audio.

After the app opens, look for the hotkey setting and the recording controls. Choose a hotkey you can remember, then make a short test recording to confirm everything works.

## 🖱️ Make Your First Recording

1. Visit the download link and get the application.
2. Open system-audio-recorder on your Windows PC.
3. Choose a hotkey for starting and stopping recordings.
4. Play the audio you want to record on your computer.
5. Press your hotkey to start recording.
6. Press the hotkey again to stop.
7. Open the recording in the trim editor.
8. Cut off any unwanted parts and save the file.

A short test recording is the fastest way to learn the app. Record ten or twenty seconds of audio, trim it, and save it. Once you know where the file goes and how the hotkey feels, longer recordings are easy.

## 🛠️ Tips for Better Results

- Make a short test recording before capturing something important.
- Check that the correct speaker or headphone device is active in Windows.
- Pick a hotkey that does not conflict with your game or other apps.
- Keep a dedicated folder for recordings so files are easy to find.
- Use the trim editor right after recording while you remember which part matters.
- Record one task at a time so files stay organized.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

If the recording is silent, make sure audio is actually playing on your PC while the recorder is running. Check that Windows is using the output device you expect.

If the hotkey does nothing, another app may be using the same key combination. Open system-audio-recorder and choose a different hotkey.

If you cannot find a saved recording, check the app’s save location and your Windows default recordings folder. Keep the app in a folder you can easily return to.

If the app does not open, download it again from the project page and run it on your Windows PC.

## 🔒 Privacy and Control

system-audio-recorder is built around system audio only. It does not capture your microphone, so private conversations in the room are not part of the recording. You control when recording starts and stops with your hotkey. Saved files stay on your computer unless you choose to move or share them.

## 🧰 Built for Windows

This is a small native Windows app built with Rust and Windows system-audio recording tools. In simple terms, it is made to be light, quick, and focused on one job: recording the sound your PC plays. It avoids a large background suite and keeps the workflow to a hotkey, a recording, and a simple trim.
